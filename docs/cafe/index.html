A small café with the best seat in town

We opened Kohvik Meri in 2019 with one idea: the window seat over the bay should come with honest coffee. Our beans come from a roastery twenty minutes away, and our bakers start at five so the first tray is warm when we open.

Dogs, laptops and long conversations are all welcome.
